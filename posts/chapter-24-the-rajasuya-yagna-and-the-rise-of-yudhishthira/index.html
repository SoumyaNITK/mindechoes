Chapter 24 - The Rajasuya Yagna and the Rise of Yudhishthira

2025-08-28

After building Indraprastha, the Pandavas ruled their part of the kingdom with justice and care. The people were happy, and the land became rich and peaceful.

Yudhishthira was loved by all. He was kind, honest, and wise. As time passed, many kings and sages praised him. He wanted to do something that would show unity and strength in the kingdom. So, he decided to perform a grand event called the Rājasūya Yagna.

What is Rājasūya Yagna?

It is a special kind of ritual that only a great emperor can do. Before performing it, the king must make sure that all other kings accept him as their emperor. If any king refuses, the one doing the Yagna must defeat them in battle.

Yudhishthira sent messages to many kingdoms to ask for their support. Most kings agreed happily. Some did not, and those would be dealt with later.

Bhishma, Krishna, and other elders blessed the idea. Krishna explained to Yudhishthira that doing the Rājasūya Yagna will bring great fame but also some dangers. Yudhishthira accepted both.

So, the preparations for the Rājasūya Yagna began. It would be a big event, with kings, sages, and guests from many places.

But before the Yagna could begin, there were some powerful kings who needed to be dealt with first. Among them was Jarasandha, the mighty king of Magadha.
