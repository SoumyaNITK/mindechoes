Chapter 26 - The Humiliation of Shishupala

2025-08-31

The Rājasūya Yagna was now ready. Kings from all parts of India came to Yudhishthira’s grand ceremony. Everyone was amazed by the beauty of the palace and the power of the Pandavas.

Krishna is Chosen as the Honored Guest

At such a great event, it was tradition to give the highest respect to the most honorable person. All the elders and kings, including Bhishma and Drona, agreed that this person should be Krishna.

Why? Because Krishna was wise, brave, kind, and had helped the Pandavas in every step. So, Krishna was given the seat of honor, and flowers were showered on him.

But not everyone was happy...

Shishupala’s Anger

Shishupala, the king of Chedi, stood up in anger. He shouted in the royal court, saying:

  • “Why should this cowherd be honored?”
  • “He is not even a real king!”
  • “This is an insult to all great rulers here!”

He insulted Krishna again and again — 100 times.

Everyone was shocked. But Krishna remained calm. He had once promised Shishupala’s mother that he would forgive 100 mistakes.

Krishna patiently counted each insult.

The Sudarshana Chakra

But when Shishupala crossed 100 insults and said the 101st, Krishna stood up.

Without saying a word, he released his Sudarshana Chakra — a magical spinning discus that never misses.

Zoooooooom...

The Chakra flew across the court and cut off Shishupala’s head in a second. His soul left his body and merged into Krishna — because deep down, Shishupala had always been connected to Krishna through fate.

What Happened After

Everyone in the court remained silent for a moment. Then they praised Krishna’s justice. Yudhishthira’s Rājasūya Yagna continued with great success.

But trouble was not over. In the shadows, Duryodhana and Shakuni were planning something dangerous...
