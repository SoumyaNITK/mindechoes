Welcome to the Mahabharata Chapter Series

2025-06-04

"What is found here can be found elsewhere. But what is not found here cannot be found anywhere else." — Mahabharata

Namaste and welcome 🙏

This is the beginning of a very special journey.
From today, I will be sharing one chapter of the Mahabharata every day, written in simple and clear English.

The Mahabharata is not just an old story.
It is a deep and powerful journey through life, dharma (duty), relationships, struggles, and truth.
Every character, every moment, and every word has something to teach us.

Why This Series?

Many people know the Mahabharata through small stories or TV shows.
But very few get the chance to read it completely, from beginning to end, in a way that is easy to understand.

That is why I started this chapter series — so that anyone, at any age, can read and understand this great epic.

Whether you are a student, a curious reader, or someone who wants to connect with Indian culture and wisdom — this series is for you.

What You Can Expect

  • 📖 One chapter every day
  • ✍️ Simple English, without missing important details
  • 📚 Complete storyline, in the right order
  • 🌱 A chance to slowly grow with the story, day by day

You can read each chapter in a few minutes. And if you follow every day, you will soon know the full Mahabharata — not just as a story, but as a guide to life.
