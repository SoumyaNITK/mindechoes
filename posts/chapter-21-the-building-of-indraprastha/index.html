Chapter 21 - The Building of Indraprastha

2025-06-25

After the division of the kingdom, the Pandavas were given a land called Khandavaprastha. It was dry, wild, and full of thorns, snakes, and demons. The Kauravas thought the Pandavas would suffer there. But the Pandavas had Lord Krishna with them — and with him, anything was possible.

A Wild Forest Becomes a Grand City

The Pandavas wanted to build a beautiful capital city on the land. So they prayed to Lord Indra, the god of rain, for help. Indra sent his divine architect, Vishwakarma, to help them. Vishwakarma was the best architect in the universe.

But before they could build the city, they had to clear the Khandava forest. It was protected by Takshaka, the king of the Nagas (serpent beings). Also, Indra himself protected the forest because his friend Takshaka lived there.

One day, a mysterious man named Agni, the god of fire, came to the Pandavas. He said, “I need to burn the Khandava forest to cure myself. But Indra always sends rain to stop me. I need Arjuna and Krishna’s help.”

The Battle with Indra

Arjuna agreed to help Agni. Agni gave Arjuna a magical bow called Gandiva, and a quiver of arrows that never ran out. Krishna became his charioteer.

A huge battle started. Indra sent heavy rains, but Arjuna shot arrows so fast that not a single drop could touch the forest. Arjuna and Krishna fought thousands of heavenly creatures, demons, and even gods. But no one could stop them.

Finally, Agni was able to burn the whole forest. He was very happy and blessed Arjuna and Krishna.

A New Friend – Maya the Demon Architect

During the burning of the forest, one demon named Maya was hiding and got saved by Arjuna. To thank him, Maya offered to build something special for the Pandavas.

He built a magical palace in the new city. It was full of wonders — walls looked like water, water looked like floors, doors appeared and disappeared. It was so beautiful that everyone was amazed.

The City of Indraprastha is Born

Soon, the wild forest of Khandavaprastha turned into a shining city called Indraprastha. It had grand roads, gardens, markets, and homes. People from all over came to live there.

Yudhishthira became the king and ruled with wisdom and kindness. Indraprastha became more beautiful and powerful than even Hastinapura.

The Pandavas were finally happy and at peace. But deep inside the hearts of the Kauravas, jealousy was beginning to grow.
