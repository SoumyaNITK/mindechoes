When Memory Wore Her Face: A Dream Etched in Silence

2025-09-08

Last night, something magical happened.

In a dream, I found myself far away from the familiar lands of Odisha — maybe in a university campus or an institute, surrounded by strangers and yet… something deeply personal stirred within me. It wasn’t just any dream. It was a bridge — a spiritual teleport — to someone I hadn’t seen in six long years.

Back when I was in 9th grade, I had a friend. She wasn’t like any other friend. She was younger than me — much younger, almost 6 years — but somehow, our connection had more depth than what people usually understand. We were spiritually in sync. I never saw her through the lens of friendship or love in the conventional sense. No, it was something more raw… more rare. She inspired me. Her presence, her energy, the way she talked — it used to lift me. And when things around me felt dull or difficult, thinking of her often brought me unexpected clarity and joy.

We shared a bond that words always fail to wrap around properly. It was top-notch. Effortless. Pure.

But life, as always, changed course. After 10th, I left school. She too shifted her school. And just like that — poof — the pages of our shared chapter seemed to close. For six years, I didn’t see her, didn’t hear from her. She existed only in my memories — a sweet corner of my heart that refused to fade.

Until last night.

In that dream, I was walking with someone, and we reached a board — written in Odia. Some announcement maybe. There were students around, speaking in Odia, from different corners of the state. I remember spotting a girl from my village among them. We exchanged recognition — that sweet, slightly awkward nod you give someone from your past.

And then… just as I was about to leave…
My eyes drifted right.

There she was.
The face I had been unconsciously searching for over six years.

Time froze. The dream blurred. But that moment? Crystal clear.

Our eyes met. There was no drama. No music. Just pure, overwhelming joy. We both smiled — wide, genuine, almost teary smiles. We shared some playful banter. Laughed. It felt like we had never been apart. Like those six years folded themselves neatly into a minute.

And then… we hugged.

It was warm. Real. Emotional.
That hug — in the dream — wasn’t just skin and touch. It was soul.
Even after waking up, I could still feel it. That emotional embrace stayed with me like a lingering fragrance. That one hug carried six years of missed words, paused memories, and silent prayers.

And you know what?
I woke up not sad. Not nostalgic.
But excited. Motivated. Happy.

Sometimes the universe gives us moments — even if just in dreams — that reignite our spark. She wasn’t just a memory anymore. She felt like a blessing once again — a subtle nudge from the universe saying: “I’m still here. You’ve still got this.”

We didn’t share a love story in the romantic sense.
We shared something even rarer: a spiritual partnership.
She was, and maybe still is, a motivational character in my life. A muse the universe once sent my way.

And even though this was “just a dream” — it felt more real than most days I live awake.

Some echoes don’t fade.
They just wait — quietly — for the right night to return.

– Gudu
