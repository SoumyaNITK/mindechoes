Chapter 22 - Narada’s Visit and the Rule of Draupadi’s Time

2025-06-26

The Pandavas were happy in their new kingdom, Indraprastha. The city was rich, beautiful, and full of peace. Everyone praised Yudhishthira for his wise and kind rule. Draupadi, now the queen, lived with all five Pandavas. But there was one big question—how would they all share time with her fairly?

Narada Muni Visits

One day, the great sage Narada Muni came to visit Indraprastha. The Pandavas and Draupadi welcomed him with respect and offered him a seat. After resting, Narada asked them a very important question:

“You all share one wife. Have you made any rules among yourselves?”

The Pandavas looked at each other. They had not really made clear rules yet.

Narada’s Warning

Narada told them a story of two brothers who shared one wife. They were deeply in love with her, but they had no rules. Slowly, jealousy and fights broke out. One day, the brothers fought so badly, they killed each other. The kingdom was ruined.

Narada said seriously,

“To avoid such a fate, you must make a rule and follow it strictly.”

The Rule of Time

So, the Pandavas sat together and made a rule. Each brother would stay with Draupadi for one year. During that year, no other brother would enter their room, even by mistake.

If anyone broke this rule, he would have to go away into exile for 12 years.

They all agreed and promised to follow this strictly.

Living in Harmony

Because of this rule, there were no fights or jealousy. Draupadi respected the time of each Pandava and gave love and care to each of them when it was their turn. The brothers stayed united and happy.

The people of Indraprastha also lived in peace and were proud of their just and wise rulers.

Key Takeaway:
Narada’s wise advice helped the Pandavas live in peace without jealousy. By following rules and respecting each other’s time, they built a strong and happy home.
