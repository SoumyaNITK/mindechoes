Chapter 25 - The Death of Jarasandha

2025-08-30

Before the Rājasūya Yagna could be done, Yudhishthira had to make sure that no powerful king stood against him. One such king was Jarasandha, the ruler of Magadha.

Jarasandha was very strong and cruel. He had defeated many kings and kept them in prison. He was planning to sacrifice them in a strange and cruel ritual.

Why Jarasandha Had to Be Stopped

Krishna told Yudhishthira that unless Jarasandha was stopped, the Rājasūya Yagna could not be done. He also said that it was not wise to attack Magadha with a full army.

Instead, Krishna had a clever plan.

The Secret Journey

Krishna, Arjuna, and Bhima disguised themselves as brahmins (holy men) and went to Jarasandha’s palace secretly. They were welcomed by the king, but soon he realized they were not ordinary men.

Krishna then told Jarasandha who they were and challenged him to a one-on-one fight. Jarasandha accepted. He chose Bhima as his opponent.

The Epic Wrestling Match

Bhima and Jarasandha fought a terrible wrestling match that went on for 13 days and nights! They were both strong and skilled. But Bhima had more energy and courage.

On the 14th day, with a secret sign from Krishna, Bhima understood how to kill Jarasandha. Jarasandha had a magical secret: he had been born in two halves, which were joined by a demon named Jara. So Bhima tore Jarasandha apart — from head to toe — and threw the two halves far away.

This time, they did not join back. Jarasandha was dead.

The Kings Were Freed

Krishna, Arjuna, and Bhima freed all the kings who had been kept in prison. They all blessed the Pandavas and promised to support Yudhishthira in the Rājasūya Yagna.

With Jarasandha gone, the biggest obstacle was removed. But a new trouble would rise… and his name was Shishupala.
